Frequently Asked

What's included?
Round-trip luxury coach transport, VIP venue parking, curated R&B tailgate with food and bar service, premium concert ticket, and onboard bottle service. Overnight accommodations included for multi-day fan experiences.
How much is it?
$2,500 per person, all-inclusive. Payment plans are available — our concierge will discuss options after your application is approved.
How many spots per coach?
Strictly 12 VIP guests per Premium Star Coach — by design. The intimacy is the experience.
When will I be contacted?
Our concierge team responds to approved applications within 24 hours with an encrypted link to lock in your deposit.
Can I book for a group?
Yes — select up to 12 guests in the party size field. Private full-coach buyouts are also available; mention it in your follow-up call.
Is my deposit refundable?
Deposits are non-refundable but fully transferable. The Vibe Escape concierge will walk you through the full policy before you commit.

Still have questions?

Email concierge@musicdept.com for white-glove support.
